Straight answers

Questions students actually ask

Is AP Calculus BC harder than AB?

BC contains everything in AB and adds series, parametric and polar calculus, and more integration techniques, at a faster pace. It is not an easier alternative — it is AB plus roughly two more units.

How do you get a 5 on AP Calculus?

Work the units in order, get the derivative and integral rules to recall speed, then practise released free-response questions under timed conditions and review every mistake by topic. The unit map above is ordered for exactly that.

Do the games cost anything or track you?

No. They are static browser builds served from this site, with no logins, installs or payments. They exist so a break stays a break instead of turning into a scroll.
